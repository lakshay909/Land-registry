- **[P2] Search State Missing URL Query Param Synchronization**
  - **Why it matters**: In [PublicSearch.jsx](file:///Users/lakshay/Desktop/Projects/Land-registry/frontend/src/pages/PublicSearch.jsx), searches and filters are stored only in component memory. Citizens cannot share a direct link to a verified parcel record with a bank or legal counsel.

  - **Why it matters**: Power users and registrars cannot use keyboard shortcuts (`/` to search, `Esc` to cancel, `Cmd+Enter` to submit) for rapid navigation.
