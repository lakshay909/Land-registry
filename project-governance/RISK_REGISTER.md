## 1. Risk Matrix

| Risk ID | Category | Description | Probability | Impact | Severity | Mitigation Strategy | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-LEG-01** | Legal / Statutory | **Claiming Digital Signatures Replace SRO Registration**: Under Section 1(4) & 3A of IT Act 2000 and Section 17 of Registration Act 1908, immovable property transfers without SRO registration are legally void. | Medium | Critical | **CRITICAL** | Clear disclaimers and explicit product positioning: on-chain EIP-712 signatures are bilateral pre-registration commitments under Sec 10A; formal SRO deed execution remains mandatory. | DH-Legal |
| **RSK-LEG-02** | Regulatory / Compliance | **RERA Real Estate Agent Non-Compliance**: Facilitating transactions in real estate project properties without Section 9 RERA registration incurs statutory penalties under Section 62 of RERA Act, 2016. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Restrict unassisted digital facilitation to standalone rural agricultural/ancestral resale plots, or obtain corporate RERA real estate agent registration for urban project listings. | DH-Legal |
| **RSK-LEG-03** | Legal / Statutory | **Remote NRI PoA Stamping Expiry**: Under Section 18 of Indian Stamp Act 1899, a Special PoA executed abroad must be stamped within 3 months of arrival in India. Unstamped PoAs cannot be registered under Section 32/33 of Registration Act 1908. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Implement automated 90-day statutory countdown timer and Tehsil Collectorate adjudication guidance for all NRI transactions. | DH-Legal & DH-Eng |
| **RSK-LEG-04** | Legal / Litigation | **Lis Pendens / Injunction Violations**: Facilitating sale of land subject to pending litigation violates Section 52 of Transfer of Property Act, 1882 and exposes buyers to voided transactions. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Integrate guided Vaad UP / RCCMS computerized case status checks and mandatory seller non-encumbrance declarations. | DH-Legal & DH-Domain |
| **RSK-FIN-01** | Regulatory / Financial | **Cryptocurrency Escrow for Real Estate**: Settling Indian domestic real estate in cryptocurrency violates RBI and FEMA regulations, exposing users to financial enforcement. | High | Critical | **CRITICAL** | Retain ETH escrow strictly on local/testnet EVMs for academic demonstration; architect INR nodal banking escrow for live transactions. | DH-Legal & DH-Arch |
| **RSK-FIN-02** | Statutory Tax | **TDS Non-Compliance under Income-tax Act, 2025**: Failure to deduct and remit 1% TDS on transactions $\ge ₹50,00,000$ under Section 393(1) and Form 141 (Schedule B) exposes buyer to statutory penalties. | High | High | **HIGH** | In build, calculate 1% TDS automatically in payment breakdowns and generate pre-filled Form 141 challans. | DH-Legal & DH-Eng |
| **RSK-INT-01** | Technical / Legal | **Government Portal Anti-Scraping & Computer Misuse**: Automated bot scraping of UP Bhulekh or IGRSUP violates portal terms and Section 43/66 of IT Act, 2000. | High | Critical | **CRITICAL** | Strictly prohibit scraping bots; deploy Assisted Citizen Workflow with client-side certified PDF ingestion and Keccak-256 hash checks. | DH-Govt & DH-Security |
| **RSK-DAT-01** | Data Protection | **Public Exposure of Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**: Committing buyer/seller Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, or phone numbers to public ledger violates DPDP Act, 2023. | High | Critical | **CRITICAL** | Zero PII on-chain. Only Keccak-256 hashes of redacted deed extracts and pseudonymous Ethereum addresses are committed on-chain. Off-chain data encrypted with AES-256-GCM. | DH-Security |
| **RSK-SEC-01** | Smart Contract Security | **Push Payment Escrow Reentrancy / DoS**: Direct push disbursement (`call{value: amount}`) in `approveTransfer` can revert if recipient is a contract, freezing administrative approvals. | Low | High | **MEDIUM** | Refactor to OpenZeppelin `PullPayment` pattern in Phase 6, decoupling authority approvals from recipient withdrawal execution. | DH-Security |
| **RSK-PRD-01** | Product / Compliance | **Agricultural Ceiling Violations in UP**: Platform facilitating agricultural land sales exceeding 5.0586 hectares (12.5 acres) without DM permission violates Section 89 of UP Revenue Code 2006. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Build ceiling limits and tenure classification checks (Bhumidhar with transferable rights) directly into listing validation workflows. | DH-Domain |
| **RSK-OPS-01** | Operational Gap | **Missing Indexer in Service Orchestrator**: `dev.sh` currently runs `server.js` and `vite`, omitting `indexer.js`, leading to event desynchronization. | High | Medium | **MEDIUM** | Update `dev.sh` to include `node backend/indexer.js` concurrently with prefixed logging during engineering phases. | DH-Eng |
