## 4. Operational Boundaries

1. **Subagents execute in read-only research mode** during Phases 0–4.
2. **No code alteration** without prior Product Owner sign-off at Phase 5.
3. Every feature dossier must be independently reviewed by a designated reviewer from **DH-QA** or **DH-Legal** before acceptance.
