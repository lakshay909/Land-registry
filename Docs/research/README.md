### Wave 2 Research Features (Active Pipeline)
- `F02 — Land Buying Journey`: Due diligence, title search, tehsil encumbrance check, token deposit, registration.
- `F03 — Land Selling Journey`: Title onboarding, Khatauni verification, asking price setting, buyer negotiation.
- `F04 — Land Records & Ownership History`: Integration feasibility with UP Bhulekh (RoR), BhuNaksha GIS, and on-chain event indexer.
- `F05 — Marketplace & Direct Buyer-Seller Discovery`: Direct listing, disintermediation legality, anti-spam protections.
- `F06 — Remote Transactions & NRI Workflows`: Non-resident Indian land management, Special Power of Attorney (PoA) authentication under UP Registration Rules.
- `F07 — Tehsil Facilitation & Verified Service Providers`: Lekhpal/document writer directory, transparent service tracking and accountability.
- `F08 — Government Valuation & Price Comparison`: UP Stamp (Valuation of Property) Rules 1997, circle rates calculation across rural/urban parcels.
- `F09 — Land Leasing (Agricultural vs. Non-Agricultural)`: UP Revenue Code tenancy provisions, lease deed registration thresholds.
- `F12 — Disputes, Fraud Prevention & Risk`: Vaad revenue court status, injunction tracking, judicial freezing (`flagProperty`).
- `F13 — Notifications & Transaction Tracking`: SMS/WhatsApp milestone alerts for rural citizens without active internet access.
- `F14 — Identity, Roles & Permissions`: OpenZeppelin AccessControl, DigiLocker KYC, role hierarchy (Admin, Registrar, Citizen).
- `F16 — Government & Third-Party Integrations`: Official API evaluation for Bhulekh, BhuNaksha, IGRSUP, and NSDL TDS portals.
- `F17 — Security, Audit & Data Protection`: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 compliance, audit trail integrity, smart contract reentrancy audits.

## 2. Research Standard & Classification Rules
All material claims must cite:
- Exact Section and Statute or Gazette notification.
- Official Government Portal URL and access date.
- Evidence Classification (`VERIFIED FACT`, `OFFICIAL PROCEDURE`, `INTERPRETATION`, `PROPOSED SOLUTION`, `ASSUMPTION`, `UNRESOLVED`, `CONFLICTING EVIDENCE`).
